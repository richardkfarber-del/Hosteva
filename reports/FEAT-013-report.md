# Technical Spike Report on Stripe Paywall Implementation

## Overview 
This report provides an analysis of the feasibility and potential implications of implementing Stripe Paywall in our system.

## Requirements and Challenges 

* Conduct research on Stripe Paywall requirements 
* Analyze market trends and competitor patch notes 
* Evaluate direct user-demand metrics for FEAT-013 viability

## Viability Assessment 

Based on the analysis, we recommend implementing Stripe Paywall to enhance user experience and revenue growth.

## Implementation Implications 

* Potential integration with existing payment gateways
* Consideration of security measures to prevent unauthorized transactions
* Regular review of market trends to ensure compliance with industry standards
