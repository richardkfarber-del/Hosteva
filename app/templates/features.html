Florida Phase I

Florida checklist depth — built for hosts, not for busywork.

Hosteva is a Florida-first short-term rental compliance research product. Enter an address, see zoning risk, work a layered municipal checklist, and open official .gov portal links when we have them. We win on curated Florida corridor depth and citation-backed tasks — not by pretending to run your inbox, calendars, or guest messaging.

What Phase I ships

  • Free Audit — Florida address lookup and preliminary zoning risk — or an honest Under Review when we do not have curated data yet.
  • Compliance Essentials ($9.99/mo or $99/yr) — Interactive state / county / municipal checklist, municipal fee notes when available, and direct .gov submission links.
  • Citation-backed rules — Checklist items point at official sources. Hosteva does not invent a green light.

What Phase I is not

  • Not a property-management system (PMS), inbox, or calendar sync product.
  • Not a live multi-channel / OTA publishing hub or “operations engine.”
  • Not legal, tax, or licensing advice — you own final filings.
  • OTA 1-click publish, multi-calendar sync, and similar ops modules stay on the Phase II waitlist.

How Hosteva fits

If you are comparing compliance tools, look for Florida municipal depth — corridors like Kissimmee, Panama City Beach, and Miami Beach — honest Under Review when a locality is not curated yet, and Essentials tasks tied to a .gov source where available. That is our wedge: careful local data, not an AI slogan and not a full property-management suite.
